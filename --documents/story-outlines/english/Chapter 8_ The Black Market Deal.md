# Chapter 8: The Black Market Deal

Once the venomous acid storm has blown over (whether successfully deflected, survived inside a rusty pipe, or paid for with a stone-dead battery), a heavy, oppressive smog hangs over the mountainous debris.

S.T.E.V.E. flickers weakly back into consciousness as you trudge through the smoking mounds of melted alloy. Suddenly, your helmet scanner chirps a sharp alert. A tiny, encrypted packet flashes across a low-band frequency.

> **S.T.E.V.E.** *(voice slightly modulated and raspy)*: "Intercepting an unsecure transmission on local comms... Hold on, this looks like an automated beacon. Someone is hawking the exact sub-orbital Navigation Core we need to steer this flying dustbin once we leave the atmosphere."
>
> **S.T.E.V.E.** *(magnifies the telemetry)*: "The coordinates point to the *Rust-Bar*, a notoriously seedy speakeasy tucked inside an old dreadnought’s manifold alley. Keep your wits about you, skipper: the patrons there trade in everything from stolen cortical stacks to irradiated snacks. The broker will want an eye-watering price for the goods."

You make your way to the manifold alley—a gloomy, neon-slashed fissure wedged between two fallen battlecruisers. Leaning against an improvised counter fashioned from corrugated titanium stands a two-headed dealer in a ratty synth-fur overcoat, frantically tapping a cracked datapad. Both heads turn to inspect you with equal measures of greed and contempt.

> **The Dealer** *(speaking in unsettling, harmonious duet)*: "Ah... the castaways. We’ve been expecting you. The Navigation Core is right here. But forget imperial credits—we require something you’re definitely carrying: either that lovely Hyper-Capacitor... or the core personality memories of your little AI mate S.T.E.V.E.!"

---

### What is your move?

* **[1] Sacrifice the Hyper-Capacitor:** Part with the capacitor with a heavy heart in exchange for the Navigation Core—you have your flight coordinates now, but no high-yield boost for your sub-light drives.
* **[2] Offer S.T.E.V.E.’s memories as payment:** Agree to the transaction, allowing the dealer to scrub a chunk of S.T.E.V.E.’s core partitions (such as his repository of bad jokes and embarrassing incident logs) to keep the hardware.
* **[3] Swindle the broker:** Pretend to agree to terms, pull a smoke canister from your belt at the crucial second, hurl it onto the counter, snatch the Navigation Core, and run for your life.

---

### What happens next?

---

#### If you choose [1] (Trade the Hyper-Capacitor)

* **What happens:** You hand over the gleaming capacitor and receive the Navigation Core. You can now calculate a safe trajectory through orbital space, but your primary engines will be woefully underpowered. You'll need to improvise a risky alternative.
* **The Catch:** You leave the bazaar empty-handed regarding engine thrust, but confident in your navigation, marching directly into **Chapter 9 (The Long March Through the Sandstorms)**.
* **Story Direction:** With flight paths plotted, you trudge off towards the howling desert in **Chapter 9**.

---

#### If you choose [2] (Format S.T.E.V.E.’s Wit)

* **What happens:** The vendor connects a data probe and wipes several gigabytes of personality routines. S.T.E.V.E. shudders, momentarily forgets the concept of dry sarcasm, but retains your hardware and secures the Navigation Core.
* **The Catch:** You keep both items, but S.T.E.V.E. now behaves with unsettling, butler-like politeness and clinical sobriety—a profoundly eerie companion for the trek to **Chapter 9**.
* **Story Direction:** Together with your disturbingly courteous AI, you head into the sandstorm in **Chapter 9**.

---

#### If you choose [3] (The Smoke Bomb Heist)

* **What happens:** The smoke grenade detonates with a loud *POP*, filling the alley with thick purple haze. You snatch the Core off the counter and sprint down the conduit as both of the dealer's heads unleash a chorus of furious gunfire into the fog. You narrowly escape, but now have half the Gryps-4 underworld pursuing your trail.
* **The Catch:** You kept all your gear and paid nothing, but angry bounty hunters are now breathing down your neck.
* **Story Direction:** Burdened by loot and pursued by armed thugs, your path leads directly into the unforgiving grit of **Chapter 9 (The Long March Through the Sandstorms)**.
