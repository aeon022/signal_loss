## The 12-Chapter Roadmap: "SIGNAL LOSS: SECTOR SCHRÖDINGER"

* **Chapter 1: The Landing Site**
  * *The Situation:* A catastrophic crash, smouldering wreckage, and your charming first encounter with locals brandishing laser pitchforks. A splendid start to the day.


* **Chapter 2: The Scrap Bazaar & the Food Truck Cartel**
  * *The Situation:* Sourcing rudimentary spare parts from deeply untrustworthy deep-space caterers serving weaponised street food.


* **Chapter 3: Night Falls (The Waiting Game)**
  * *The Situation:* S.T.E.V.E. enters low-power standby mode. You must twiddle your thumbs in real time while the reactor cools down, listening to unsettling scuttling noises in the pitch black.


* **Chapter 4: The Labyrinth of the Glitch Nomads**
  * *The Situation:* Navigating an irradiated canyon of corrupted data where reality itself pixelates, logic throws a tantrum, and syntax errors can erase your kneecaps.


* **Chapter 5: The Holy Rubbish Heap**
  * *The Situation:* Infiltrating the Temple of the Saint-Plastic Recycling Monks to nick the legendary Hyper-Capacitor straight off their sacred altar.


* **Chapter 6: The Act of Sabotage**
  * *The Situation:* An obnoxious rival scavenger ship descends to loot your hard-earned rubbish. Time for a spot of tactical defence.


* **Chapter 7: The Acid Rain Surprise**
  * *The Situation:* A torrential storm warning of industrial-grade paint stripper forces a frantic camp evacuation. The hull is sizzling nicely.


* **Chapter 8: The Black Market Deal**
  * *The Situation:* Rendezvous with a delightfully shady informant who holds the coordinates to the elusive fuel canister—yours for an extortionate moral or material price.


* **Chapter 9: The Long March Through the Sandstorms (Real-Time Timer)**
  * *The Situation:* A dreary slog on foot towards the planet’s only functional filling station. Your terminal link drops out periodically just to keep things tense.


* **Chapter 10: The Repair Crisis**
  * *The Situation:* You’ve actually got all the bits, but S.T.E.V.E. throws a digital wobbler and demands an admin password you definitely forgot three years ago.


* **Chapter 11: The Final Countdown**
  * *The Situation:* The engines are purring (or rather, coughing violently), but every faction on the planet senses your departure and charges the launchpad en masse.


* **Chapter 12: The Leap into the Unknown**
  * *The Situation:* Blast-off. Depending on your questionable life choices over the preceding days, you either achieve glorious freedom or blunder headfirst into the next interstellar shambles.
