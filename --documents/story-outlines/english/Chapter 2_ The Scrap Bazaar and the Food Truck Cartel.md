# Chapter 2: The Scrap Bazaar and the Food Truck Cartel

Once the festive chatter of the laser pitchforks fades into the distance, S.T.E.V.E.’s aggressively flashing red warning icon drags you back into grim reality. The atmosphere tastes of ozone, melted plastic, and the bitter tang of stale, bottom-shelf coffee substitute.

> **S.T.E.V.E.:** "Well done, Captain. We’re still respiring. Unfortunately, my power gauge is shrinking faster than the annual budget of a bankrupted space agency. If we have any ambition of leaving this cosmic dump alive, we desperately need scrap to patch the hull, and fresh juice for my logic gates—otherwise, I might genuinely forget your name, which would be a tragedy for both of us."

You heave yourself upright and survey your surroundings. In the middle distance, past precarious heaps of buckled satellite dishes and fractured bulkheads, a garish neon sign flickers with spastic enthusiasm: **"B.B.Q. & BATTERIES – THE ONLY FAST GRUB IN THE SECTOR"**.

A colossal, heavily reinforced van rests precariously on four rusted hydraulic stilts in the mud. A jet of raw flame burps from its improvised exhaust into the bruised purple sky. Behind the counter, an imposing quadrupedal alien in a grease-stained apron is enthusiastically polishing a meat skewer the size of a lamppost, rhythmic to the beat of punishing, low-budget acid techno.

> **S.T.E.V.E.:** "Ah, exquisite timing. The Intergalactic Food Truck Syndicate. They cook with weapons-grade plutonium and flog off toxic industrial offal as local delicacy. Yet, by some cruel irony, they possess the one thing we need: a half-depleted fusion cell with just enough kick to keep our life support ticking over for three days."

The chef catches sight of you, locks all four rheumy yellow eyes on your frame, and brings a butcher’s cleaver down onto the counter with a sound that suggests very little room for polite haggling.

---

### What is your move?

* **[1] Stagger up to the hatch:** Attempt an honest transaction with your hard-earned **Scrap (`SCRAP`)**, despite the chef looking like a fellow who would happily toss you onto the grill if the exchange rate displeases him.
* **[2] The covert flank:** Slither around the back of the van under the cover of thick, rancid diesel fumes and try to pinch a leaky spare cell directly from the auxiliary generator.
* **[3] The promotional distraction:** Have S.T.E.V.E. blast a dreadfully ear-splitting, cheesy 1980s commercial jingle for your crashed ship through your suit speakers to bewilder the chef completely.

---

### What happens next?

---

#### If you choose [1] (Walk up and trade honourably)

* **What happens:** You empty your meagre collection of junk onto the grease-caked counter. The four-armed chef inspects the rusted offerings, gives them a sniff, and bares an alarming array of jagged fangs. He slides a crackling fusion battery across the counter, accompanied by an unsolicited, luminescent green alien burger for which he charges you a scandalous surcharge.
* **The Catch:** You have your battery, but your scrap pouch is completely cleaned out, and you are now the proud owner of a glowing, mildly radioactive sandwich that hums in B-minor.
* **Story Direction:** With power restored, darkness begins to blanket the wasteland. You are propelled straight into **Chapter 3 (Night Falls)**, where sub-zero temperatures force you to batten down the hatches.

---

#### If you choose [2] (The five-finger discount)

* **What happens:** You creep through the billowing greasy smoke towards the back of the truck. You successfully pry a heavy, ancient battery from the generator cradle. However, on your retreat, you step square on an empty tin can—*CLATTER!* The chef spins around, discharges a glob of superheated plasma that singes your backpack, and you are forced to run for your miserable life through the scrapheaps.
* **The Catch:** You acquired the battery for free, but S.T.E.V.E. reports severe thermal damage to your suit, and the chef’s blood-curdling curses echo across the canyon.
* **Story Direction:** You escape with your prize, but you are now a wanted fugitive, blundering wounded into the freezing horrors of **Chapter 3**.

---

#### If you choose [3] (S.T.E.V.E.’s marketing extravaganza)

* **What happens:** S.T.E.V.E. dials his external speakers up to eleven and belts out an astoundingly terrible commercial for a non-existent scrap dealership. The chef is so profoundly bewildered by this brazen musical atrocity that he nearly drops his cleaver in hysterics. He flings the battery at your chest as a "promotional courtesy" and merely demands a digital autograph from S.T.E.V.E.
* **The Catch:** You walk away unscathed with your scrap intact, but S.T.E.V.E.’s ego expands to such insufferable proportions that his commentary becomes borderline intolerable.
* **Story Direction:** Fully powered and accompanied by an insufferably smug AI, you prepare for sunset and the chilly ordeal awaiting in **Chapter 3**.
