# Chapter 11: The Final Countdown

The reactor of the *CSS RUST-404* vibrates with bone-jarring violence, shaking the very bedrock beneath your boots. Through the cracked cockpit glass, you see the smog over the junkyard ignite into an angry crimson flare, accompanied by an ominous rumble echoing across the basin.

Unfortunately, you do not have the landing pad to yourself.

> **S.T.E.V.E.** *(practically bellowing into your earpiece)*: "Captain! Full red alert! Every disgruntled local on Gryps-4 has caught wind of our departure! The laser-pitchfork brigade is cresting the northern ridge, the Food Truck Syndicate is rolling in with their up-armoured food wagon, and I’d bet my last resistor that Vex the pirate is inbound on our six!"
>
> **S.T.E.V.E.** *(strobing in catastrophic crimson)*: "They are charging the launch apron! They intend to board us before the sub-light manifold achieves flight pressure. We have precisely sixty seconds until the primary thrusters ignite. You must secure the boarding hatch from the outside or force an immediate lift-off!"

Out past the hatch, you can already make out armed figures charging through the red dust towards the ship. You stand at the helm—hands black with soot, adrenaline hammering in your chest.

---

### What is your move?

* **[1] Blow the hatch explosive bolts:** Pull the emergency jettison lever to blast the heavy airlock door outwards, catching the charging mob in the shockwave and bowling them over like tenpins.
* **[2] Floor the throttle on a reckless early launch:** Disregard all pre-flight diagnostic routines, shove the main throttle straight into maximum overdrive, and risk disintegrating on the launchpad just to blast away from beneath their feet.
* **[3] Transmit an Imperial distress flare via S.T.E.V.E.:** Use the last drops of emergency bandwidth to broadcast a fraudulent, chaotic high-priority distress signal to the Imperial Sanitation Fleet, inviting them to orbital-bombard the entire sector while you lift off.

---

### What happens next?

---

#### If you choose [1] (Explosive Hatch Jettison)

* **What happens:** *BOOM!* The heavy titanium airlock door detaches with extreme prejudice, hurtling into the front rank of charging aliens. They tumble into the dirt in a tangle of limbs and laser pitchforks while you dive frantically into the cockpit and slam the internal bulkhead shut.
* **The Catch:** You halted the assault in the nick of time, but the ship's side is now wide open to the elements, welcoming a rather brisk crosswind.
* **Story Direction:** The thrusters roar into full burn, catapulting you straight into the epic finale of **Chapter 12 (The Leap into the Unknown)**!

---

#### If you choose [2] (The Reckless Early Launch)

* **What happens:** You slam the throttle forward. The *CSS RUST-404* shrieks like an enraged mechanical beast, breaks free from its makeshift moorings, and punches through the toxic sky in a towering column of fire, leaving the baffled scavengers coughing in your exhaust smoke.
* **The Catch:** A thoroughly cinematic and triumphant exit, but the premature thrust places catastrophic mechanical stress on your freshly patched hull.
* **Story Direction:** With a glowing aft section and an alarming pulse rate, you breach the lower atmosphere—straight into **Chapter 12**.

---

#### If you choose [3] (The Imperial Distress Signal)

* **What happens:** S.T.E.V.E. broadcasts the distress code. Instantly, massive orbital targeting lasers from the Imperial Sanitation Division slice through the clouds, blanketing the landing pad in precision scrap-clearing ordnance. The opposing factions scatter in sheer terror.
* **The Catch:** You cleared the runway with supreme efficiency, but stray shrapnel from the orbital bombardment peppers your own ship's fuselage.
* **Story Direction:** Amidst the beautiful, chaotic fireworks, you ignite the main drive and streak upward into **Chapter 12**.
